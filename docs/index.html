How it works

Four steps, about 10 minutes

The app's setup guide walks you through each one. You only do this once per TV.

1

Turn on Developer Mode on the TV

This lets the TV accept Nuvio from your phone. Grab the TV remote:

  1. Press Home and open Apps.
  2. Press 12345 on the remote.
  3. Switch Developer Mode to On.
  4. In Host IP, type the IP the app shows you. Select OK.

No number keys on your remote? Press the 123 button and pick the numbers on screen.

3

Find your TV

Tap Find my TV. The app looks for Samsung TVs on your Wi‑Fi and checks the connection the moment you pick one.

Not in the list? Type its IP. On the TV: Settings › General (or Connection) › Network › Network Status › IP Settings.

If the router gives your TV a new IP one day, the app finds it again by itself.

4

Sign in with Samsung, then install

Samsung TVs only open apps made for that TV. Tap Sign in with Samsung, sign in on Samsung's page, and come back. The app does the rest:

  1. Checks the phone, the TV and Developer Mode.
  2. Downloads Nuvio and checks the file.
  3. Signs it for your TV and installs it.

It takes about a minute. You see every step as it happens. You sign in only once per TV.

Updates

New version? One tap.

When Nuvio TV gets an update, your phone tells you. Tap the notification and the TV is updated. No sign-in, no setup again.

  • Or let it update by itself. Turn on "Update Nuvio automatically" and new versions go on the TV when it's on and on the same Wi‑Fi. If the TV is off, it tries again later.
  • See what's new before you update, right on the card.
  • Beta versions are there too, marked "beta". A TV only gets betas if you pick one.
  • The app updates itself too. Tap Update and confirm when your phone asks.

Setup guide
No guessing

If something's wrong, it says what to fix.

Before every install, the app runs 7 quick checks, one by one. It stops at the first one that fails and shows you how to fix it, step by step, with the exact numbers to type.

  • Phone got a new IP? The most common snag. The app spots it and shows the new IP to type on the TV.
  • TV got a new IP? The app finds the TV again and switches by itself.
  • Still stuck? Tap Report a problem. It makes a bug report with your TV IDs and e‑mail removed.
More in the app

Small things that save you time

More than one TV

Tap + to add another TV. Switch between them in one tap. Each TV gets its own update notification.

Type on TV

Type into the TV's keyboard from your phone. Handy for searching in Nuvio or filling in sign-ins.

Backup made for you

The app saves a backup file to your Downloads folder. Keep it, so updates still work after you change phones. You can add a password.

Nuvio Legacy, if you want it

An unofficial community version that tends to run smoother on 2018–2020 TVs. It installs next to Nuvio TV.

Used the desktop installer?

Import its certificate file, and the app keeps updating the Nuvio that's already on your TV.

Private by design

You sign in on Samsung's own page. The app never sees your password. No analytics, no tracking.

FAQ

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. The app is free and open source (GPL-3.0). Nuvio TV is free too.

Do I need a computer?

No. Everything happens on your phone and your TV.

Do I need a Samsung account?

Yes, a free one. Samsung TVs only open apps made for that TV, and only Samsung can make that certificate. You sign in once, on Samsung's own page in your browser. The app never sees your password.

It worked before, now it can't reach the TV. Why?

Most often your phone got a new IP from the router. The TV only accepts the Host IP you typed in Developer Mode. The app shows you the new IP to type.

To stop this happening, give your phone a fixed IP. The app shows you how when it notices the change.

Nuvio TV or Nuvio Legacy?

Nuvio TV is the official app and the one we recommend. Nuvio Legacy is an unofficial rewrite by a community developer (iqui27/nuvio-native-legacy) that can run smoother on older TVs. It needs a 2018 or newer TV. You can have both.

What about LG TVs?

Not supported yet. It's planned.

Is this an official Nuvio or Samsung app?

No. TV Installer is made by Pratyush. Nuvio is made by NuvioMedia. This app does the same job as the Samsung part of the desktop Nuvio TV Installer, on Android.
